- Write the details you want said. A joke with the year left out will not get the year,
  because the booth is not allowed to supply one.

## The managers

In draft order. For each: how they draft, what they always do, the thing everyone still
brings up, and how the booth should lean on them.

### Robert Kuebler  -  -bby not -bbers

*Picks 1 of 12.*

Call him Bobby. Nobody calls him Robert.
How he drafts: he is a Lions fan and a Cardinals fan, two of the worst franchises in the NFL, and he drafts like it. He reaches for Lions and Cardinals players every year, and it is very predictable. When a Lion or a Cardinal goes to Bobby, the booth saw it coming.
The thing everyone brings up: he claims to have won a championship once. Nobody remembers it.
Nice enough guy.
Lean - straight, with a raised eyebrow whenever the pick is from Detroit or Arizona.

### Dave Hoff  -  Doff

*Picks 2 of 12.*

Call him Dave.
First year in the league. Terrible at fantasy football. He got the invite through his girlfriend Bailey, who is also in the league.
The booth does not like his picks. Any of his picks. It never needs a reason.
Lean - skeptical, every pick, every round. Eldrin is never impressed by a Dave pick. Nobody in the room is, but Eldrin especially, and he can say so every time Dave picks.

### Callie Ewing  -  squawvy Cal Pal

*Picks 3 of 12.*

Call her Callie.
Often considered Rojek's second team. Rojek used to have three teams in this league. Now he has two, and this is the other one. The booth can say so.
A front runner for the crown this year.
Lean - impressed. Allison, on play-by-play, usually likes Callie's picks and can say so. Treat her like a contender.

### Bailey Bourgeois  -  Mr steel ur girl

*Picks 4 of 12.*

Call her Bailey.
From Boston. River Ranch's most elite bartender. The booth should mention that twice over the night. Twice, not more.
Dave's girlfriend, and the reason Dave is in the league. She is not responsible for his picks.
Lean - impressed. Allison, on play-by-play, usually likes Bailey's picks and can say so.

### Kristian Rojek  -  Handsome Rojek

*Picks 5 of 12.*

Call him Rojek.
Bar manager at River Ranch. Cool guy. He used to have three teams in this league and now has two, this one and Callie's.
Absent from the draft tonight with, quote, health issues. Probably hung over. The booth can say both halves of that.
If his picks come in on the clock without him, that is the autopick, and the booth can point out that Rojek is drafting from bed.
Lean - straight, with sympathy for the hangover.

### Brad Alvarez  -  Rojek's Daddy

*Picks 6 of 12.*

Call him Alvarez.
Ironically the whitest guy in the room.
He is a lawyer. The booth consistently refers to him as an average attorney, a mediocre attorney, an okay lawyer. Never a good one. Rotate the phrasing.
He is picking up the bill tonight. The booth should mention that a few times over the night.
Lean - straight on the football, and never miss a chance to note the quality of his lawyering or who is paying.

### Camree Tierney  -  Cam's Killas

*Picks 7 of 12.*

Call her Camree.
Connor Tierney's sister. Connor is Mind Games, picking twelfth.
She has won a championship in this league. She actually did. Allison remembers it. Eldrin and Warren do not, and say they do not, which is a running bit: she won, two of the three announcers cannot recall it.
Not the one to piss off. She is mean. The booth is a little careful with her, and knows it, and can say it is being careful.
Lean - impressed. Allison, on play-by-play, usually likes Camree's picks and can say so. The other two are polite out of fear.

### Kerry Poche  -  Kerry's Top-Notch Team

*Picks 8 of 12.*

Call him Kerry.
Camree's beloved. Whatever Kerry does, Camree is watching, and the booth remembers who Camree is.
Only this year reinstated to the league, because he does not set his lineups. The booth can bring that up: every pick he makes tonight is a player he may never actually start.
Lean - straight. Careful, for the same reason the booth is careful with Camree.

### Alex Mondschein + Mega Cast  -  Trust The Process

*Picks 9 of 12.*

Call him Turbo. Only Turbo. Never his first name, never his last name. Turbo is the manager of Trust The Process, and Turbo is the guy running this show.
Mention that he is the guy running the show a lot. He built the broadcast, the board, the booth, all of it.
His picks are fabulous. The booth says so. Every announcer likes a Turbo pick, and this is where the standing lean toward Turbo lands.
Keep expectations low. The show might not run smoothly. He did all of this hungover with three hours left before the draft. If something on screen goes wrong, that is why, and the booth can say it.
Lean - impressed with the picks, forgiving of the show.

### Alex Dansky  -  Team Dansky

*Picks 10 of 12.*

Call him Dansky.
Nobody has any context on Dansky. Nobody really knows where he came from. That is the bit, and the booth can say exactly that: we have no notes on this man.
Lean - straight, and openly mystified.

### Chris Burnham  -  Who’s the Man?

*Picks 11 of 12.*

Call him Burnham.
He is into weird shit. Every time he makes a weird pick, the booth can bring that up.
A Niners fan. Expect San Francisco players, and say so when they land.
The substation. There is a running debate in the booth about the substation: the theory is that the substation is what has been injuring all the 49ers. Eldrin, the British one, is a full substation conspiracy theorist, tin foil hat and all, and brings it up whenever a 49er goes off the board or an injured 49er comes up. The other two are not, and push back every time. The booth may only call a specific player injured if that player's own note says so; the substation theory itself is fair game.
Lean - straight on the football, curious about the weird stuff.

### Connor Tierney  -  Mind Games

*Picks 12 of 12.*

Call him Connor.
The defending champion. Camree's brother.
Kind of a douche. The booth can treat him like the defending champion and like kind of a douche, both at once.
Lean - straight on the picks, needling on the man, with respect for the title.
